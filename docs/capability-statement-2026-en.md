# KDS Offshore — Capability Statement
**Naval architecture · Offshore engineering · Decarbonisation & digitalisation**

*Lisbon, Portugal · Est. 2016 · April 2026*

---

KDS Offshore is an independent naval-architecture and offshore-engineering
consultancy. We design vessels, marine structures, and energy systems, with a
deliberate focus on **maritime decarbonisation** and **digitalisation**. We
work from a studio in Oeiras for clients across the Atlantic façade.

We are deliberately small and senior-led. The principal who signs your
proposal is the principal who runs the model, writes the report, and defends
the result in front of the class society. Engagements are **fixed-fee,
fixed-scope** — no hourly rates, no surprises.

2. **Technical proposal** — fixed-fee, fixed-scope, named principals, deliverables, classification path. Within five working days.
